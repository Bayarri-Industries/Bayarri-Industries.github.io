Research & Development

Specter is Bayarri Industries' most ambitious system — a quantum radar platform designed to detect and track stealth aircraft, low-observable drones, and any target specifically engineered to be invisible to conventional radar.

Conventional radar works by bouncing electromagnetic waves off a target. Stealth technology defeats that by absorbing or deflecting those waves. Quantum radar works on an entirely different principle — one that stealth absorptive coatings cannot counter.

The Technology

Specter is built on quantum illumination — a technique that uses pairs of quantum-entangled photons. One photon is sent toward the target. The other is retained. When the transmitted photon interacts with anything in the environment, the entanglement creates a measurable correlation with the retained photon that classical noise cannot replicate. The result is detection capability that operates below the noise floor of conventional systems.

Stealth coatings absorb radar waves. They cannot absorb quantum correlations. That is the fundamental advantage Specter is built to exploit.

Why Bayarri Is Building This

The proliferation of stealth technology across near-peer adversaries has created a detection gap that conventional radar cannot close. The U.S. and its allies need a system that makes stealth irrelevant. Specter is that system.

Lucas Bayarri is pursuing a dual degree in Computer Science and Quantum Mechanics specifically to build this. The physics is being studied. The architecture is being designed. Specter is not a product on a roadmap — it's the reason the roadmap exists.

The Orion Integration

Specter doesn't just detect. It feeds raw quantum radar data into Orion, which classifies the target, predicts trajectory, and routes the intelligence to whatever response system is connected. Detection without interpretation is just noise. Specter and Orion together close that gap — from raw signal to actionable threat intelligence in one pipeline.

That integrated system — quantum radar feeding an autonomous AI — is what puts Bayarri Industries in a class of its own.
