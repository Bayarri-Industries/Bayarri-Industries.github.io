Orion

The brain behind every Bayarri system. Built from scratch. Runs everything.

Active Development

Orion is not a product. It's the infrastructure. Every system Bayarri Industries builds — Sentinel, Specter, and everything that comes after — runs on Orion. It is the AI layer that ties the entire ecosystem together.

Most AI systems today are wrappers. A model someone else trained, an API someone else built, dressed up in a new interface. Orion is none of that. Every weight, every layer, every architectural decision was made from scratch by a single person with a clear vision of what defense AI needs to be.

What It Is

Orion is a custom 136 million parameter transformer — built in PyTorch, trained on domain-specific knowledge, and designed to reason across multiple intelligence domains simultaneously. It combines a custom neural architecture with a TinyLlama language backbone and a retrieval-augmented generation system that grounds every answer in verified knowledge.

On top of that sits the Consciousness Loop — an autonomous cycle that runs continuously in the background. Orion perceives its environment, thinks through a Chain-of-Thought and Reflexion process, acts on gaps in its knowledge, and reflects on what it learned. It doesn't wait to be asked. It learns on its own.

Why It Matters

The goal is simple: one AI that gets smarter over time, understands the battlefield, and powers every Bayarri product. When Sentinel identifies a threat, Orion classifies it. When Specter detects an object, Orion predicts what it is and where it's going. Orion is the thing that makes Bayarri Industries more than a collection of tools — it's what makes it a system.

Where It's Going

The current focus is coding domain mastery — getting Orion to 9/10 quality on technical reasoning before expanding to other domains. Once that benchmark is hit, the system expands to cybersecurity, physics, signals intelligence, and eventually full battlefield awareness. Every contract Bayarri wins funds the next stage of Orion's development. The flywheel only spins one direction.
