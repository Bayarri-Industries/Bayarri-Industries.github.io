Orion

The brain behind every Bayarri system. Built from scratch. Runs everything.

Active Development

Orion is not a product. It's the infrastructure. Every system Bayarri Industries builds — Sentinel, Specter, and everything that comes after — runs on Orion. It is the AI layer that ties the entire ecosystem together.

Most AI systems today are wrappers. A model someone else trained, an API someone else built, dressed up in a new interface. Orion is none of that. Every weight, every layer, every architectural decision was made from scratch by a single person with a clear vision of what defense AI needs to be.

What It Is

Orion is a custom 235 million parameter transformer — built in PyTorch from scratch, trained exclusively on domain-specific knowledge across 9 intelligence domains. No pretrained weights, no external backbone — every layer, every parameter, every architectural decision built from first principles. The architecture mirrors modern frontier models: RMSNorm, SwiGLU feed-forward networks, and a 32,768-token BPE vocabulary — the same components powering today's leading LLMs, applied entirely to Bayarri's specialized knowledge base.

On top of that sits the Consciousness Loop — an autonomous cycle that runs continuously in the background. Orion perceives its environment, thinks through a Chain-of-Thought and Reflexion process, acts on gaps in its knowledge, and reflects on what it learned. It doesn't wait to be asked. It learns on its own.

Why It Matters

The goal is simple: one AI that gets smarter over time, understands the battlefield, and powers every Bayarri product. When Sentinel identifies a threat, Orion classifies it. When Specter detects an object, Orion predicts what it is and where it's going. Orion is the thing that makes Bayarri Industries more than a collection of tools — it's what makes it a system.

Where It's Going

Orion V2 is actively training across all 9 intelligence domains simultaneously — math, coding, systems, AI architecture, electronics, physics, quantum, aerodynamics, and security. An autonomous research pipeline feeds it continuously: when Orion encounters something it doesn't know, it queues the topic at high priority, crawls credible sources, and updates its own knowledge base without any human intervention. The target is 90%+ benchmark parity with commercial models on domain-specific tasks — then surpassing them where it counts: specialized domains, battlefield inference, and scenarios no public dataset was ever trained on. The flywheel only spins one direction.
