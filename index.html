Leadership

Lucas Bayarri

Founder & CEO  ·  19  ·  Boca Raton, FL  ·  U.S. Army 35T

Most defense companies are run by suits who haven't touched hardware since the Cold War. Lucas Bayarri started Bayarri Industries at 19 because he got tired of waiting for someone else to build what he could already see in his head.

Inspired by the idea that one person with the right technology can change the battlefield, Lucas is currently hand-building Orion — a custom AI system designed from the ground up, not assembled from someone else's API. No shortcuts. No pretrained wrappers passed off as innovation. Every parameter trained, every architecture decision made from scratch. Because if you're going to build the brain of a defense ecosystem, you build it right.

He's pursuing dual degrees in Computer Science and Quantum Mechanics — not because a counselor recommended it, but because Specter demands it.

Serving in the U.S. Army as a 35T — Military Intelligence Systems Maintainer/Integrator — Lucas is inside the machine. Every broken system, every inefficiency, every moment he thinks why doesn't this exist — that's the next Bayarri product.

The goal isn't to compete with Lockheed Martin, Boeing, or Raytheon. It's to make them irrelevant.

Bayarri Industries is the company Lucas would've wanted to exist. So he's building it.
